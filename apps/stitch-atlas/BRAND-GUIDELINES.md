# Stitch Atlas — Original Brand Identity, v1

**Positioning:** A premium, welcoming, ad-funded English-language crochet learning journal and free-pattern publication. Not a pattern marketplace, reseller, crafting e-commerce business, or imitation of Chevron Pattern.

**Name:** Stitch Atlas (working brand name; domain not purchased or cleared for trademarks).  
**Voice:** Warm, intelligent, precise, optimistic, never "AI craft factory" or commercially pushy.  
**Tagline:** Crochet · Creative Living.  
**Editorial signature:** “There’s beauty in every little loop.”

## Typography

- Hero headlines: editorial humanist serif, fallback Georgia / Iowan Old Style / Baskerville.
- Interface, navigation, labels: approachable sans, fallback Avenir Next / Segoe UI.
- Rhythm: pronounced display vs small uppercase section numbers; long guides at 15–17px with open line spacing.
- Avoid decorative script and large uppercase paragraph blocks.

## Nonnegotiable editorial rules

1. All new real pattern pages must have physically tested original instructions with auditable stitch counts, appropriate photos, gauge, sizes and clear terminology.
2. Never copy other designers’ patterns, illustrations, product photography or watermarked images.
3. Never claim real testimonials, ratings, tested status, number of patterns or newsletter subscribers without evidence.
4. Do not auto-publish from Rankstien's current food Recipe pipeline. No Recipe JSON-LD on crochet pages.
5. Display-ad strategy may use reserved slots and respect mobile reading. Do not install ad scripts before publisher approval and explicit privacy controls.
6. Keep preview noindex until real domain, substantial verified content, policy pages and reliable real-world data exist.
