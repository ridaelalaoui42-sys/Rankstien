# RankStein Fresh Campaign Pipeline

Updated: 2026-08-24

This is the permanent default campaign contract for RankStein, Hermes, Odysseus, and MCP-backed agents.

## Output Contract

For every selected keyword on every ready domain, the system should produce:

- One researched Spanish recipe article published to that domain.
- One article hero generated by Codex `gpt-image-2` and uploaded to Supabase Storage.
- One primary Pinterest pin created from the hero image and uploaded with a verified `pin_id` or `pin_url`.
- 30 article-specific Pinterest assets produced from exactly 15 unique scraped Pinterest sources. Each source yields exactly one deterministic `viral_visual` pin and one deterministic `recipe_card` pin.
- Exactly 30 unique Pinterest queue jobs for the validated current-run assets and configured account identity.
- A completion report with article URL, hero image path/URL, primary pin path/URL, remaster target, remaster count, missing count, queued job count, domain handle, board, and account handles.

## Pipeline

1. Start services with `python rankstein.py launch`.
2. Refresh domain-aware keyword intelligence with `refresh_trend_keywords` / `rankstein.py trends`; an empty qualified queue automatically invokes this research path.
3. Discover upcoming candidates from Pinterest Trends/Search only. Google News, Google Trends RSS, and Google Suggestions may validate an exact Pinterest-origin phrase, but may never inject or rewrite a candidate. Reject vague phrases, require specificity plus independent demand evidence, then select only `Pending` keywords present in fresh qualified `daily_best_keywords.json` evidence across all ready domains.
4. Search AgentMemory for domain, account, selector, and source-quality lessons.
5. Scrape source articles with `scrape_news_sources` and require at least two independently relevant recipe sources before writing.
6. Reject generic source drift before and after extraction; source candidates must match the actual keyword, not only broad words such as "ideas" or the domain niche.
7. Extract compact source notes with `extract_article_content`. If fewer than two relevant sources survive, stop before article writing. Do not carry full articles into prompts.
8. Generate article JSON with source notes, full Recipe schema, visual prompts, negative prompt, and Spanish alt text using Codex directly or an explicitly attested Hermes OpenAI-Codex route. Only when Codex is unavailable may the worker try a configured free model through the maintained Hermes CLI. Rejected Codex content, provider-attestation failures, and quality/schema failures do not permit fallback. Direct Gemini, Odysseus, OpenCode, OpenRouter, and deterministic article generation are never allowed; provider failure stops publication.
9. Validate with `validate_article_quality`.
10. Generate the hero image with the generated `hero_image_prompt` using Codex `gpt-image-2` only. Fail closed on any Codex failure.
11. Upload hero to Supabase Storage with `upload_image_to_supabase`.
12. Build the final article payload with `build_supabase_content`.
13. Create the primary hero-based Pinterest pin with `create_article_pin`.
14. Upload the primary pin with `automation_upload_pin_direct`; when the supervisor is healthy this creates a priority-1 queue job and waits for the supervisor-owned browser result. Require a verified `pin_id` or `pin_url`.
15. Publish/upsert the article with `publish_article_to_supabase` and link the pin ID.
16. Launch `run_article_remaster_campaign` for the article.
17. The remaster campaign generates a short dish/ingredient scrape brief and must accept exactly 15 unique, relevant Pinterest pin sources. If fewer survive, it creates no variants, queues nothing, and reports incomplete; generated/native source fill is forbidden. Each accepted source becomes exactly two 1000x1500 assets: an image-led `viral_visual` and a readable `recipe_card` containing the article's real ingredients and numbered preparation steps. Pairs are atomic: if either variant fails, neither is queued. The exact 30 current-run assets are validated and enqueued with source, pair, variant, domain, and run identity.
18. The Pinterest supervisor uploads queued remaster jobs across configured accounts.
19. Mark the keyword `Live` only when Supabase publish/storage, verified primary `pin_id` or `pin_url`, 15 unique scraped sources, 30 paired assets, and 30 unique queue job IDs are all proven.
20. Write a domain/run-scoped article remaster report under `data/reports/campaigns/`. A report is successful only when identity matches the current article/run, `source_counts` is Pinterest 15/native 0, both variants exist for every unique source, and the enqueue result proves 30 unique jobs.

## Keyword Research Gate

- Candidate origin: Pinterest Trends/Search only. Google News, Google Trends RSS, and Google Suggestions may validate the exact Pinterest phrase, but Google-only candidates are rejected. Reports retain Pinterest provenance and exact validation sources; strict production runs record `candidate_origin_policy=pinterest_required`.
- Precision: a candidate must name a recognizable dish plus a concrete ingredient, style, origin, occasion, or cooking technique. Category-only and generic phrases such as `postres faciles`, `ideas de comida`, or `pollo facil` are rejected.
- Demand: each precise survivor must meet `RANKSTEIN_MIN_SEARCH_DEMAND_SCORE` (default `2`). The built-in Google autocomplete depth is labeled `search_demand_score`; it is not literal monthly search volume.
- True monthly volume: Google Trends reports normalized relative interest, not absolute monthly searches. Exact average monthly searches require a configured Google Ads `GenerateKeywordHistoricalMetrics` provider. Reports must not claim monthly volume without that provider.
- Freshness: Google News hits may improve ranking but cannot replace the demand gate. `daily_best_keywords.json` expires after `RANKSTEIN_KEYWORD_EVIDENCE_MAX_AGE_HOURS` (default `36`).
- Failure policy: provider failures are isolated, but no Pinterest candidate provenance, no external demand signal, malformed/stale evidence, or no qualified survivors means no roadmap authorization and no article worker launch. Static seed fallback is disabled.

## Scrape Brief Rule

The Pinterest image scraper must not invent or hardcode search keywords internally. It must search the actual article title/dish, not a broad roadmap keyword. The campaign layer generates and passes:

- `search_query`
- `search_queries`
- `core_terms`
- `min_core_matches`
- `expected_terms`
- `blocked_terms`
- `domain_handle`
- `category`
- `brand_notes`

The scraper enforces that brief, rejects irrelevant/undersized images, deduplicates Pinterest pin IDs, and reports per-query diagnostics. If fewer than 15 unique relevant Pinterest sources survive, it must stop before composition and queue mutation. Native/generated source fill, crops, and clones cannot satisfy production source slots.

The scraper searches all generated query variants, deduplicates pin IDs, and scores image alt text plus card title, description, board, and link metadata. Every accepted pin must satisfy the generated dish-specific `min_core_matches` threshold. Cooking-method words such as `horno` or `freidora de aire` are context, not core dish identity. Category, niche, or brand-context terms are supporting signals only.

## Paired Pin Studio Contract

- Source target: exactly 15 accepted, dish-relevant, unique scraped Pinterest pin IDs.
- Output target: 30 assets, exactly two per source.
- `viral_visual`: photo-led save/click composition with the configured domain's colors, short hook, brand, public domain, and CTA. Do not invent ratings, cooking times, or social proof.
- `recipe_card`: photo plus the article's real title, ingredients, numbered preparation steps, optional chef tip, brand, public domain, and CTA.
- Both assets are vertical 2:3 and deterministic typography is applied after the source image is acquired.
- A pair is complete only when both variants are present. Incomplete pairs are deleted and never enqueued; queue mutation requires all 15 complete pairs together.
- Queue jobs and campaign reports retain `pair_id`, `source_index`, `variant`, `variant_label`, `source_batch`, `domain_handle`, and `pipeline_run_id`.
- The dashboard groups both variants under their source, previews them in a secure lightbox, and displays live source-pair and asset counts.

## Image Provider Default

RankStein article heroes are Codex-only:

- Provider: `create_hero_image_codex` with `gpt-image-2` through Hermes/Codex OAuth.
- Failure policy: no Pollinations, scraped, or programmatic placeholder image may be published as an article hero.
- Quality: `RANKSTEIN_CODEX_IMAGE_QUALITY=medium` by default; accepted values are `low`, `medium`, and `high`.

## Article Provider Default

RankStein article creation is Codex-first and fail-closed outside the approved Hermes free-model availability fallback:

- Default primary path: `RANKSTEIN_ARTICLE_PROVIDER=hermes-codex` with `RANKSTEIN_HERMES_CODEX_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8642/v1`; Hermes must attest `openai-codex` and a GPT model.
- Optional direct path: Codex CLI when explicitly configured.
- Availability-only fallback: one configured free model through the Hermes CLI/provider routing; it is not a direct OpenRouter or other provider API call.

An unreachable Codex route may use the approved free Hermes fallback. Invalid JSON, schema failure, provider-attestation failure, or quality failure leaves the keyword failed or `Needs Verification` and publishes nothing. Direct Gemini, Odysseus proxy, OpenCode, OpenRouter, and deterministic/template article text are not production fallbacks.
