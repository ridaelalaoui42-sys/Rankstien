# Brand Voice Guide: Recetadolce

**Mission:** To empower home bakers with reliable, sophisticated, yet approachable sweet recipes that bring the "dolce vita" into every kitchen. We strip away the fluff to focus on what matters: flavor, technique, and success.

---

## 1. Brand Personality: The "Dolce" Spirit
*   **Expert but Humble:** We understand the science of baking, but we explain it like a friend over coffee, not a chemistry professor.
*   **Warm & Indulgent:** Our language reflects the joy of sweets. We don't just bake "cakes"; we create "velvety crumbs" and "golden indulgences."
*   **Efficient:** We respect the baker's time. We skip the 2,000-word personal memoirs and get straight to the oven.

## 2. Tone of Voice: Concise, Practical, Helpful
Following the **Directo al Paladar** style, we prioritize utility over SEO-fluff.

*   **Concise:** Use active verbs and tight sentences. 
    *   *Avoid:* "It is generally recommended that you might consider preheating your oven."
    *   *Use:* "Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F)."
*   **Practical:** Focus on sensory cues. Tell the reader what to look, smell, and feel for.
    *   *Example:* "Whisk until the peaks are stiff and glossy—they should hold their shape when you lift the beaters."
*   **Helpful:** Anticipate "The Error." Every recipe should include a "Baker’s Note" addressing the most common mistake for that specific dish.

## 3. Language & Grammar
*   **Vocabulary:** Use evocative but precise terms: *Fold, temper, zest, emulsion, crumb, aeration, decadent, balanced.*
*   **Units:** Always provide **Metric (grams)** as the primary measurement for precision, with Imperial (cups) in parentheses for accessibility.
*   **Formatting:** Use bold text for key temperatures and timings to make the article "scannable."

## 4. Content Structure (900–1,500 Words)
To maintain the "Concise but Helpful" standard, every post follows this hierarchy:

1.  **The Hook (150 words):** Why this recipe is the "ultimate" version. 
2.  **The Master Technique (200 words):** The one professional secret that makes this recipe work (e.g., "The secret to a crack-free cheesecake").
3.  **Ingredients Spotlight:** Quick bullets on quality—why European butter or Dutch-processed cocoa matters here.
4.  **Step-by-Step Instructions:** Numbered, logical, and focused on the *action*.
5.  **Variations & Storage:** How to make it vegan/gluten-free and how to keep it fresh for day two.

## 5. The "Recetadolce" Don'ts
*   **No "SEO-Souls":** Never repeat the keyword "Best Chocolate Cake" 50 times.
*   **No Vague Instructions:** Avoid "Bake until done." Instead, use "Bake for 25-30 minutes until a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean."
*   **No Clutter:** If a photo doesn't demonstrate a technique or show the final texture, it doesn't belong in the post.
