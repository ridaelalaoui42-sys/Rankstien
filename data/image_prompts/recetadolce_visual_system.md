# RecetaDolce Visual System

## Brand Direction

RecetaDolce should feel like an editorial Spanish dessert atelier: refined, warm,
handcrafted, and appetizing without looking like generic stock photography.

The visual language is built around:

- Ivory limestone, warm parchment, old marble, matte ceramic, brushed copper.
- Deep cherry red, pistachio green, cocoa brown, cream, olive oil gold.
- Soft natural window light with realistic shadows and food texture.
- Carefully plated Spanish and French-influenced recipes.
- Crop-safe compositions for responsive blog cards and article heroes.

Avoid:

- Text inside generated images.
- Watermarks, logos, labels, brand names, fake magazine covers, or UI overlays.
- Random unrelated props, duplicate dishes, messy restaurant clutter.
- Overly dark, blurred, synthetic, glossy, or obvious stock-photo styling.
- Reusing one generic plate image across unrelated recipes.

## Global Hero Prompt Template

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: RecetaDolce blog recipe hero, 16:9 landscape
Primary request: {recipe title and exact dish}
Scene/backdrop: editorial Spanish dessert atelier, ivory stone or warm wood surface,
matte ceramic servingware, subtle linen, minimal refined props.
Subject: {specific finished dish}, styled as the hero subject with visible texture,
garnish, and the ingredient cues named in the article title.
Style/medium: photorealistic premium food photography for a sophisticated recipe
blog, not stock photography.
Composition/framing: landscape hero crop, three-quarter or overhead angle as fits
the dish, main dish centered with generous crop-safe margins.
Lighting/mood: soft natural side light, warm bakery atmosphere, realistic shadows,
polished but edible.
Color palette: ivory, cream, deep cherry, pistachio green, cocoa, caramel, olive gold.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no hands, no duplicate
dishes, no unrelated props.

## Category Prompt Template

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: RecetaDolce category hero/card image, 16:9 landscape
Primary request: {category name} category image represented by a curated arrangement
of 2-4 finished desserts or dishes from that category.
Scene/backdrop: refined Spanish pastry table with ivory stone, parchment, ceramic,
linen, and one restrained accent color.
Subject: category-defining foods, clearly recognizable and appetizing.
Composition/framing: editorial still life, crop-safe, enough negative space around
the edges for category cards.
Lighting/mood: soft natural light, elegant, warm, premium.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no hands, no crowded buffet.

## Pinterest Pin Prompt Template

Primary request: vertical premium food image for {recipe title}.
Scene/backdrop: same RecetaDolce atelier styling, with clean negative space in the

Lighting/mood: bright natural light, vivid texture, elegant.
